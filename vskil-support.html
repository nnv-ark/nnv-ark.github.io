VSKiL — Support

A native macOS app for preparing and filing Icelandic VAT returns (VSK-skýrsla) with Iceland Revenue (Skatturinn).

Getting started

  • Add your company in Settings → Companies: name, ID number (kennitala) and VAT number. If you use FELAG, VSKiL reads your companies from there.
  • Save your company's web key (veflykill) from Iceland Revenue in Settings. It is kept in your Mac's Keychain, never in the app's data.
  • Pick a period and add sales and purchases with New Entry (⌘N), or import the VAT summary from RUKK with From RUKK… or by dragging the file onto the window.
  • Click Fetch Details, then Validate, then File VAT Return. The receipt and payment claim appear in the app.

Common questions

Can I use VSKiL in English? Yes. It follows your Mac's language, and you can choose English or Icelandic in Settings → General → Language.

Can I keep more than one company? Yes. Each company has its own periods, entries and web key, and a RUKK summary is only ever imported into the company whose ID number it carries.

Where did my window go? VSKiL is a single-window app: closing the window quits it, and your work is saved as you go. Open VSKiL again to continue.

Want to try it without real data? Sample files and a walkthrough are at nordnordvestur.com/vskil-demo.
