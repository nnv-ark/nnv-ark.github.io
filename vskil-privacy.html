VSKIL — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

VSKIL is designed to keep your business data on your own Mac. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.

What the app does with your data

VSKIL prepares Icelandic VAT returns (VSK) from the sales and purchase entries you type in. When — and only when — you press Skila VSK, the report is sent directly from your Mac to the official web service of the Icelandic tax authority, Ríkisskattstjóri, over encrypted HTTPS. The report contains your company's kennitala, VAT number and the amounts of the period, authenticated with your own web key (veflykill). The data travels between you and the tax authority only. We never see it.

Information we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies are used.
  • No invoice data, kennitala, VAT numbers, or web keys are ever sent to NNV ehf. or any third party.
  • We do not use third-party data collection SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Company names, kennitala, VAT numbers, invoice entries, and filed amounts are stored locally on your Mac so the app can work. If you use the FELAG company register, the app reads the shared companies.xml file directly from its folder on your Mac after you grant access once — the file never leaves your device. All of this information stays on your Mac (and in your own backups, if you make them) and is removed when you delete the app and its data.

The web key (veflykill)

Your web key from Ríkisskattstjóri is stored in the macOS Keychain, the operating system's encrypted credential store — never in the app's data files. It is used solely to authenticate your submissions to the tax authority's web service.

Ríkisskattstjóri's web service

Filing a VAT return connects you to a service operated by the Icelandic tax authority using your own credentials. How the tax authority processes the data you submit is governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

Children's privacy

VSKIL is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
