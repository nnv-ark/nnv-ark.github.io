FELAG — sample files

Sample company register for reviewing FELAG. The three companies and all their details are fictional.

Easiest way: the built-in sample data

  1. Open FELAG.
  2. Choose Help → Load Sample Data.
  3. A yellow banner appears at the top of the window: Sample Data — fictional details, not your real records. Everything below it is sample data.
  4. To return to your own records, click Leave Sample Data in the banner (or Help → Leave Sample Data). FELAG keeps no real records unless you enter them.

  1. Download companies.xml (click it above).
  2. In FELAG, choose File → Import Company File… (⇧⌘I) and pick the file.
  3. FELAG reports how many companies it added. Importing only ever adds — a company already in the register (same ID or same ID number) is left alone.

What to look at

  • The sidebar lists the companies, each with its mark. Use the + and − buttons below the list (or File → New Company, ⌘N) to add and delete companies.
  • The checkmark marks the active company — the one our other apps read. Select a different company and click Make Active to change it.
  • The detail form holds name, ID number (kennitala), VAT number, address, contact details, bank account, a short note and a logo. Every change is saved as you type — there is no Save button and no unsaved-changes state.
  • Validation: type an invalid ID number (for example 4503158950) or a bank account that is not 12 digits, and the field is marked red with an explanation. Valid input shows the formatted values (450315-8850, 0301-26-123456).
  • Logo: click Choose Logo… and pick one of the PNGs above, or drag an image straight onto the logo field.
  • Connections (View → Connections, ⌘T, or the FELAG badge at the bottom of the sidebar) shows the four apps that share this company register: RUKK, VSKiL, LAUNA and BLIZZ.

About the Connections panel

FELAG stores your company details once, on your Mac, and the four apps above read them from that one place. Those four are separate apps, sold separately, so on a Mac where they are not installed the four cards stay grey and say Not installed. A card turns green after that app has run on the same Mac at least once and registered itself. Nothing is missing from FELAG in that state: the register, the form, the validation, the logo and the sample data all work on their own. RUKK is already on the App Store if you would like to see a companion app.

Two things worth knowing

  • FELAG is a single-window app: closing the window quits it, and your changes are already saved. Open FELAG again to continue.
  • If the window does not come back after you close it, use the Dock or Launchpad — FELAG is not running any more at that point.
